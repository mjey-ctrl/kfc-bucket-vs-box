KFC Bucket vs Box: What Can ₱500 Get You?

Your budget is ₱500, your friends are starving, and everyone’s asking the same question: Bucket or box?

Order too little, and someone ends up counting the last piece of chicken. Order too much, and your wallet takes a hit for leftovers nobody planned on eating.

Whether you’re treating yourself, grabbing lunch with a friend, or sharing a meal with your barkada, choosing the right KFC order can make all the difference. Here’s how to make the most of your budget!

Which one is more sulit?

It depends on what you’re looking for! If you’re eating alone or want your own complete meal, a box or individual meal is a practical choice. But if you’re eating with friends or family, a bucket can make sharing easier.

Quick checklist before you order

  • Count how many people are eating.
  • Decide between sharing (bucket) and individual meals (box or meal).
  • Set aside part of your ₱500 for sides or drinks.
  • Confirm prices on the official KFC menu.

Frequently asked questions

Is a KFC bucket cheaper than a single meal?

It depends on the bucket size, price, and the individual meals you’re comparing it to. Calculate the total cost and determine how many people each option can serve.

Can ₱500 feed four at KFC?

Yes, if everyone is okay with a limited order. Four 1-Pc Chicken Ala Carte orders would cost ₱460 according to our menu. However, this does not provide everyone with a full meal.
